Halloween Tablecloths FAQs

What size Halloween tablecloth do I need?

Measure the table first, then add the drop you want. A 54 x 108 inch cloth covers a standard 6-foot folding table with a short drop on each side; a 60 x 84 inch cloth suits a 4- to 6-seat dining table. Round tables need a round cloth — a 70 inch round fits most 4- to 6-seat round tables.

Disposable or reusable — which is better?

Plastic and PEVA cloths are the easy-clean choice: wipe them down or toss them after the party. Polyester and microfiber cloths cost more up front but are machine-washable and reusable, so they win if you host every year.

Can Halloween tablecloths go outdoors?

Check the listing before it goes outside. Plastic and PEVA prints handle a covered porch fine, but lace and fabric cloths should stay dry, and any cloth near candles or a fire pit needs to stay well clear of open flames.

How do I make a plain table look Halloween-ready fast?

Layer them. A solid black or purple cloth underneath with a lace spider-web overlay on top gives you two textures for the price of one look, and it hides stains from the candy bowl better than a single printed cloth.
