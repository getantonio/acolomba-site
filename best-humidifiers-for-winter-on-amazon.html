Before you buy a humidifier for winter

Warm mist or cool mist for winter?

Warm mist feels cozier on cold nights and can be soothing during cold-and-flu season, but the unit itself runs hot — cool mist is the safer choice with small children or pets. Both raise room humidity equally well.

How do I keep a humidifier clean?

Empty and refill the tank daily rather than topping off old water, and deep-clean weekly with white vinegar to prevent mineral buildup and mold. Filterless and dishwasher-safe designs make this much easier.

What size humidifier do I need?

Match the coverage rating to your room: 150–300 sq ft for bedrooms, 400–750+ sq ft for living rooms or open plans. An undersized unit will run constantly without ever hitting your target humidity.
