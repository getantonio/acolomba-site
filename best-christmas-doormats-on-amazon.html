Christmas Doormat FAQs

Coir or linen — which doormat material is better for winter?

Coir is the workhorse: coarse coconut fibers scrape mud and snow off shoes, and most coir mats here have a vinyl or PVC backing to keep them in place. Linen mats are slimmer and lower-profile, so they fit better under doors and in mat holders.

What size Christmas doormat should I get?

The standard single-door mat is 17x30 inches, and most picks here use it. For double doors or a wide entry, step up to a 24x36 or 24x48 mat so the mat spans the full opening.

Can Christmas doormats stay outside all winter?

Most of these are built for indoor and outdoor use, but coir lasts longest in a covered entry — extreme moisture and sunlight wear it down faster. Shake, sweep, or lightly hose coir mats clean; check each listing's care instructions.

What is a layered doormat look?

A smaller coir welcome mat placed on top of a larger patterned rug, like the plaid set here. The rug adds color and size, the coir mat does the scraping — and you can swap the top mat each season while keeping the rug.
