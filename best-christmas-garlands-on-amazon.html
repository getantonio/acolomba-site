Christmas Garland FAQs

What length Christmas garland do I need?

Measure the mantel, staircase rail, or doorway first, then add a little slack for draping. Standard mantels suit the 5.9- to 6-foot garlands here; 9-foot garlands cover long mantels and stair rails; the 18-foot option here is for porches, long staircases, and front doors that need one continuous run.

Should I buy a pre-lit or unlit garland?

Pre-lit garlands save the stringing step and come with timers on several picks here. Unlit garlands, like the cedar pick on this list, suit decorators who want their own lights, a specific light color, or a natural daytime look with no wiring.

Can Christmas garlands go outside?

Several garlands here are rated for indoor and covered or sheltered outdoor use, and the 18-foot pick is built for porches and front doors. Check each listing's rating — an indoor-only flocked garland will not hold up to rain.

How do I store a Christmas garland?

Coil it loosely rather than folding it tightly, keep flocking away from damp storage, and bag battery boxes separately with the batteries removed so they do not corrode over the year.
