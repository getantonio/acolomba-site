Christmas Stocking FAQs

What size Christmas stocking should I buy?

Most stockings here run 18 to 20 inches long, which fits small gifts, candy, and stocking stuffers with room to spare. The keepsake needlepoint style is smaller at 8 x 17 inches — better as a personalized mantel piece than a big-gift holder.

Can personalized stockings be washed?

It depends on the material. Knit and burlap stockings here are generally hand- or machine-washable per their listings, while needlepoint and velvet styles should be spot-cleaned or handled gently — check the individual listing's care instructions before washing.

How many stockings come in a set?

Sets on this list range from 2 to 6 stockings. A 6-pack covers a large family mantel in one purchase, while singles like the needlepoint and monogram styles are meant to be bought one per person.

Do Christmas stockings come with a way to hang them?

Yes — every stocking here has a hanging loop, rope, or ribbon. For a heavy mantel display, use a proper stocking holder rather than a nail or adhesive hook.
