Christmas Throw Pillow FAQs

Do these Christmas pillows come with inserts?

Most picks here are covers, not filled pillows — they are made to slip over 18 by 18 inch inserts you already own, which makes swapping them in December and storing them in January easy. The personalized snowman pick is the exception: a complete 14 by 14 inch pillow.

How many pillow covers do I need for a sofa?

A set of four covers is usually the best value for a full sofa refresh, while a set of two works for a single chair or for layering with pillows you already have. Mix one patterned set with one solid or plaid set rather than matching everything.

Are Christmas pillow covers machine washable?

Nearly all of these covers have hidden zippers and are machine washable, but check each listing's care label before washing — faux fur and tufted covers prefer a gentle cycle.

Can Christmas pillow covers stay out after Christmas?

Buffalo plaid, snowman, and tree prints read as winter decor through January, so the plaid and tree picks here can stay out after the ornaments come down.
