Halloween Garlands FAQs

How long a garland do I need for a mantel?

Measure the mantel or railing, then add a little slack for swag. Most garlands here run 6 to 9 feet; a standard mantel needs about 6 feet, while a porch railing or staircase usually wants 9 feet or a 2-pack.

Battery or plug-in — which lighted garland is easier?

Battery garlands win for mantels and doorways with no outlet nearby. Check the battery count before you buy — most lighted garlands here take 3 AA or 3 AAA batteries, not included — and look for a 6-hours-on timer so you are not switching it on and off every night.

Can Halloween garlands go outside?

Lighted willow-vine garlands with a waterproof battery box handle a covered porch, but paper and cardstock banners should stay indoors. Unlighted fabric and silk garlands are the safest bet for anywhere rain might reach.

How do I make a garland look full instead of thin?

Hang the lighted garland first, then layer an unlighted one in front of it. The lights glow through the leaves and cutouts, and two garlands read as one full display instead of a single thin line.
