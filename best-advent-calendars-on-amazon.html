Advent calendar FAQs

Are advent calendars just for kids?

Yes. About half of this list is aimed at grown-ups: tea, jam, beauty, candle and luxury-chocolate calendars all sell out to adult buyers every year.

When should I order an advent calendar?

Popular editions like Bonne Maman and the LEGO advent calendars sell out well before December. Buying in October or early November is the safe move.

Are any advent calendars reusable?

Some are, and reusable wooden calendars are a classic way to fill your own treats year after year. The calendars in this roundup are all single-season, treat-filled editions.
