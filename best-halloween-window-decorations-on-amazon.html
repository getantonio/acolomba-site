Window-decoration questions, answered

Static cling or adhesive decals &mdash; which is better?

Static clings stick with water alone, peel off cleanly, and go back in the box for next year &mdash; the top picks on this list. Adhesive decals grip harder on textured glass but take more effort to remove; check the listing if you're renting.

Will these work on the outside of the window?

Most clings are made for the inside surface (they read through the glass). LED window scenes and hanging decorations work from either side as long as they stay dry &mdash; keep battery packs away from rain.

How do I make window silhouettes glow at night?

Put a backlight behind them: the LED silhouette scenes on this list glow on their own, but for plain black silhouettes a floor lamp or backlight kit a few feet behind the glass turns them into glowing shadow scenes the whole street can see.
