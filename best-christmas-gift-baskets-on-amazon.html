Christmas Gift Basket FAQs

What is the best Christmas gift basket for someone you barely know?

A classic food basket is the safest bet — the Harry & David fruit and nut basket or the Hickory Farms meat and cheese box suit almost any adult and any occasion, which is why they have been holiday staples for decades.

How much should you spend on a Christmas gift basket?

It depends on the relationship, not the basket. These picks range from smaller snack baskets and coffee sets to grand meat-and-cheese boxes — pick the size that matches the occasion and let the basket do the presenting.

Do Amazon gift baskets ship directly to the recipient?

Yes — at checkout on Amazon you can ship to the recipient's address and add a gift message, so the basket arrives ready to open. Check each listing's delivery estimate before ordering, especially in December.

Are gift baskets a good corporate Christmas gift?

Yes. Food baskets are the classic corporate gift because they suit a whole office — the Bonnie & Pop chocolate platter and the six-tier nuts tower on this list are sized for sharing, and most of these sellers offer business gifting options.
