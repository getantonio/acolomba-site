Espresso Machines FAQs

How much pressure (bar) do I actually need?

True espresso extracts at 9 bars, and nearly every home machine advertises 15 or 20 — that headroom is normal and harmless. Don't shop by the bar number alone: the grinder, fresh beans, and temperature stability change your cup far more than 15 vs 20 bars on the box.

Semi-automatic vs super-automatic — which should I buy?

Semi-automatics give you control over grind, dose, and tamp — the fun route with a learning curve. Super-automatics grind, tamp, and brew at one touch. Capsule machines are the fastest and easiest, but cost the most per cup. Pick by how much ritual you want in your morning.

Do I need a separate grinder?

For a semi-automatic, eventually yes — pre-ground coffee goes stale fast and limits what the machine can do. Machines with built-in grinders hit the sweet spot for beginners; super-automatics always include one. Whatever you choose, buy whole beans and grind fresh.

How do I keep it tasting good year after year?

Descale every 1-3 months depending on your water, backflush a semi-automatic weekly, and rinse any milk frothing system after every use — dried milk ruins flavor and clogs steam wands. Use filtered water and fresh beans; a maintained $150 machine beats a neglected $700 one.
