Before you buy Christmas wrapping paper

How much wrapping paper do I actually need?

Check the total square footage, not just the roll count. A standard shirt box takes roughly 4 square feet, so a 120 sq. ft. set covers around 25–30 average gifts, while a 175 sq. ft. jumbo roll stretches further. Odd shapes and big boxes eat paper fast — when in doubt, buy the bigger set.

What does “reversible” mean?

Each roll carries a different design on each side, so one roll gives you two looks. It is the easiest way to make a pile of gifts look coordinated without buying twice as much paper.

Why do some listings mention grid lines?

Grid or cut lines printed on the reverse side of the paper act as a cutting guide, so you can cut straight edges without a ruler. Nearly every Hallmark set on this list includes them.
