Before you buy a giant Halloween inflatable

Will it survive a windy October night?

Inflatables stay up with ground stakes, tether ropes, and often sandbags — most listings on this page include the full anchor kit. In high wind, deflate and store the inflatable rather than letting it tear; the blowers are designed for continuous outdoor use but severe storms are a different story.

How much power and yard space does a 12-footer need?

The built-in blowers run on a standard outdoor outlet and are low-wattage (LED lighting sips power). Measure your yard first: a 12-footer needs roughly a 12x12-foot patch with clearance above, plus 5–10 feet of cord reach to the nearest outlet.

How do you store one after Halloween?

Most of these are 210T–210D polyester or Oxford fabric with reinforced stitching — they pack down into a storage bag roughly the size of a sleeping bag. Dry the fabric before folding it away to prevent mildew, and store it somewhere rodents can't chew through it.
