Halloween Zombie Decorations FAQs

What's the best type of zombie decoration for outdoors?

Groundbreakers, inflatables and lawn stakes are built for the yard — look for weather-resistant plastic and stake-down designs. Sound-activated animatronics work outdoors too, but keep their battery boxes and electronics sheltered from heavy rain.

How do sound-activated zombie props work?

A built-in sensor listens for nearby noise — footsteps, voices, a slammed car door — then triggers the motion, lights and sounds. Most run on AA or AAA batteries (usually not included), so stock up; a busy Halloween night drains them fast.

Can I use zombie decorations with a fog machine?

Yes — low-lying fog plus zombie groundbreakers is the classic graveyard look. Set the fog machine low and slightly upwind so the mist drifts across the props. Keep fog fluid and moisture away from battery compartments and plug-in animatronics.

How do I store zombie decorations after Halloween?

Let everything dry fully, remove batteries from animatronics to prevent corrosion, and deflate inflatables flat before folding. Store latex pieces like severed heads away from heat and sunlight, which crack the material over time.
