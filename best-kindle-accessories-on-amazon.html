Kindle Accessories FAQs

Do Kindle accessories fit every Kindle model?

No — cases and screen protectors are cut for specific models and generations, so check your Kindle's generation and screen size (6", 6.8", 7", or 10.2" Scribe) before buying. Page turners, book lights, stands, and USB-C cables are generally universal.

Will a magnetic case drain my Kindle's battery?

Official Amazon cases and most quality third-party cases use magnets only to trigger auto wake/sleep — they do not power the case, so there is no battery drain from the magnets themselves.

Can I charge my Kindle with any USB-C cable?

Yes — current Kindle models (11th/12th gen) charge over USB-C, so any USB-IF certified USB-C cable works. Amazon no longer includes a wall adapter, so pair the cable with a charger you already own.

Is a page turner worth it for a Kindle?

If you read in bed, in a bath caddy, or propped on a stand, a clip-on RF page turner lets you flip pages without reaching the screen — a small gadget that makes hands-free reading genuinely effortless.
