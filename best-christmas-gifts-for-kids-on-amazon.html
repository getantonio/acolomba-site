Before you buy Christmas gifts for kids

How do I pick a Christmas gift that's right for a kid's age?

Match the gift to the kid's age, not the kid's wishes alone. Building sets and art kits shine for ages 5–8, STEM kits and card games hit the sweet spot around 7–12, and instant cameras, speakers, and tablets are teen territory. Every product on this list has its manufacturer-recommended age printed right on the listing, so check that before you add it to the cart.

How early should I order Christmas gifts for kids from Amazon?

Order by the second week of December to be safe. The most-wanted toys sell out and get restocked in waves, and shipping windows get tight in the final week before Christmas. Ordering early also gives you time to swap anything that arrives damaged without the holiday panic.

Are tech gifts actually safe for kids?

Yes, as long as you buy the version made for kids — that means parental controls, kid-proof cases, and content filters. The kids-specific products on this list (the tablet, the smart speaker, and the e-reader) all include parental controls and worry-free replacement guarantees, which is exactly why they are on this list instead of their grown-up equivalents.
