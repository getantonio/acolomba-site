Halloween Pumpkin Decorations FAQs

When should I put up Halloween pumpkin decorations?

Most decorators start in late September to early October so the display is ready well before trick-or-treat night. Faux and light-up pumpkins are reusable, so putting them out early just means more nights of glow.

Are light-up pumpkin decorations safe to use outdoors?

Battery, solar, and low-voltage LED pumpkin lights stay cool to the touch and carry no fire risk like real candles. Look for IP44 or higher waterproof ratings on anything staying outside in autumn rain.

How do I stop an inflatable pumpkin from blowing away?

Every quality inflatable ships with ground stakes, tether ropes, and sand or water bags. Stake it on soft ground, fill the bags, and it will ride out blustery October nights.

Can I reuse faux pumpkin decorations next Halloween?

Yes — that's the whole point. Foam, plastic, and fabric pumpkins pack flat or stack in a tote and come back year after year, unlike real pumpkins that collapse within weeks.
