Christmas Village Set FAQs

Should I start with a complete set or buy houses one by one?

Start with a complete set. A boxed town gives you houses, trees, lights, and figures in one purchase, so the display looks finished on night one — then you can add single collector pieces like the Lemax clock tower here year by year.

Battery or plug-in village sets — which is better?

Battery sets go anywhere without hunting for an outlet, which is why most of the complete towns here are battery-powered. Plug-in animated scenes, like the Bdor train village, suit a permanent mantel spot where the train and music run all evening.

What size village set fits a mantel?

Measure first: the wooden tiered village here is narrow and tall for tight shelves, while the 20-piece MOMENTS IN TIME town spans about 20 inches wide. White ceramic sets in the 4 to 6-inch house range fit almost any mantel.

Do village sets work as gifts?

Yes — complete sets are among the easiest Christmas gifts for collectors and decorators alike, because the recipient gets a finished scene, not a single piece they have to build around. The gift-ready boxed towns here are made for exactly that.
