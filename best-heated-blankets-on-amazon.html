Heated blanket FAQs

Are heated blankets safe to sleep under?

Modern heated blankets are built for it: nearly every blanket on this list has an automatic shut-off (from 1 to 12 hours), overheat sensors, and third-party safety certification (ETL or UL). Still, follow the care label, never use a damaged or bunched-up blanket, and unplug it when not in use.

Can I wash a heated blanket?

Almost all of them — every blanket here is machine washable. Detach the controller and cord first, wash on a gentle cycle, and never submerge the controller in water.

How much does a heated blanket cost to run?

Very little. Most run around 100W, roughly one-thirtieth the power of a space heater, so warming yourself under a blanket is far cheaper than heating the whole room.
