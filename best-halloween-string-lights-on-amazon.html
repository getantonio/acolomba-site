String-light questions, answered

How long should a Halloween string light strand be?

For a porch rail or doorway, 20&ndash;30 feet with 50&ndash;100 lights is plenty. For bushes or a full-yard scene, look for 60+ feet &mdash; several picks on this list connect end-to-end, so you can chain strands without another plug.

Are these lights safe to leave outside?

Outdoor-rated strands on this list are built water-resistant for patios and yards; always check the listing's indoor/outdoor rating before hanging. Battery strands avoid cords entirely &mdash; handy for pumpkins and places with no outlet.

What's better for Halloween: orange or purple?

Orange reads classic Halloween; purple reads haunted-house. Most of the strands here come in both (or mixed), and the novelty pumpkin and bat sets glow their own warm light &mdash; pick by the mood of your display.
