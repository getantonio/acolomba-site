10 Best Halloween Dog Costumes on Amazon (2026) FAQs

How do I pick the right costume size for my dog?

Fitwarm lists XXS through XXL with chest-size charts — measure your dog's chest and back and prioritize the chest measurement. Zoo Snoods sizes run from 3-lb Chihuahuas up to 180-lb large breeds, so check the weight ranges on the listing.

Are Halloween dog costumes safe?

Avoid anything your dog can chew off or choke on, skip restrictive designs if your dog hates clothes, and never force a costume. Hoods, ruffled collars, and loose parts should be supervised — the Pet Krewe Oscar the Grouch costume, for example, should be hand washed and is best as a photo costume rather than all-night wear.

Will a costume keep my small dog warm on Halloween night?

It depends on the fabric — the Fitwarm pajamas and pumpkin fleece hoodies add genuine warmth for small dogs, while lightweight satin capes like the vampire cloak are pure costume. If your dog runs cold, the knit Zoo Snoods pull-overs cover ears and neck and add warmth.
