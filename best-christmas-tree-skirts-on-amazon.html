Christmas Tree Skirt FAQs

What size tree skirt do I need?

Measure your tree's stand, then pick a skirt at least a few inches wider than it on every side. A 48-inch skirt is the standard pick for a 6 to 7.5-foot tree, while a 32 or 36-inch skirt suits smaller trees and tight corners — both sizes appear on this list.

How do you put a tree skirt on without lifting the tree?

Every skirt here opens at the back — a slit with ties or a hook-and-loop closure — so you wrap it around the trunk and fasten it, no lifting required. The faux fur and velvet picks close with hook-and-loop tape; the knits use ties.

Faux fur, velvet, or knit — which looks best under a tree?

Faux fur reads as fresh snow and hides lumps in the stand best. Velvet looks the most formal under the lights. Cable knit is the cozy farmhouse pick, and it pairs well with rustic ornaments and a plaid ribbon.

Can a tree skirt stay out all winter?

Yes — faux fur, velvet, and knit skirts all read as winter decor, not just Christmas decor, so the plain white and cream picks here stay out happily through January.
