Halloween Throw Pillow FAQs

Do Halloween throw pillow covers come with inserts?

Usually not. Most Halloween throw pillow covers — the 18x18 sets on this list included — are covers only, so check the listing before you buy and have inserts ready. A few picks here are filled plush pillows that arrive ready to display, which the product cards call out.

What size pillow insert fits an 18x18 cover?

An 18x18 cover fits an 18-inch insert. For a fuller look, some decorators size up to a 20-inch insert inside an 18-inch cover — the slight squeeze makes the pillow look plumper and the print sit tighter.

Can I wash Halloween pillow covers?

Most polyester covers are machine washable on a gentle cold cycle — check the listing's care instructions first. Wash seasonal covers after Halloween and store them flat, and they will be ready to haunt again next October.

How do I style Halloween pillows without overdoing it?

Mix one statement pillow with neutrals. Pair a bold print — skulls, bats, or a 3D pumpkin — with plain black, orange, or beige pillows so the Halloween design pops instead of cluttering. Swap the covers out after the season and the base pillows stay year-round.
