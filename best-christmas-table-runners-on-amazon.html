Christmas Table Runner FAQs

What size Christmas table runner fits my table?

Measure the table length first. A 72-inch runner suits tables seating 4 to 6; 90 to 108 inches covers 6 to 10 people; the 144-inch embroidered runner here is for banquet or buffet tables. Let the runner drape a few inches past each end for the finished look.

Are Christmas table runners machine washable?

Most polyester and faux-linen runners here are machine washable — check each listing. Faux-fur and sequin styles need gentler care: the gold sequin runner is hand-wash only, and the faux-fur sequin runner can be hand or machine washed.

What is the difference between a table runner and a tablecloth?

A runner is a narrow strip down the center of the table that frames the centerpiece, while a tablecloth covers the whole surface. Runners work over a bare table or layered on top of a tablecloth.

Can I use a Christmas table runner after December?

Many can. Plaid, velvet, and sequin runners in neutral patterns work for winter parties and New Year's tables, while nutcracker, reindeer, and Christmas-tree prints are best saved for the holiday season.
