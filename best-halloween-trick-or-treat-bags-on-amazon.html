How much candy can these bags actually hold?

Tote-style bags in the 10–13 inch range hold a serious haul — most kids fill them before they wear out. The mini pails and 50-packs are for handing out candy at your own door or table décor, not for carrying the night's loot.

Are LED trick-or-treat bags safe for kids?

The LED bags on this list glow rather than heat — the lights are battery-powered strips or button cells in sewn compartments, and the glow doubles as visibility on dark streets. Standard batteries go in the usual place; follow the age guidance on the listing.
