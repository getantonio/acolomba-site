Standing Desks FAQs

What height range do I need in a standing desk?

Measure your standing elbow height first. A 28–46 inch range fits most people from about 5'2" to 6'1"; if you are taller, look for a 48-inch-plus maximum like the VIVO or FlexiSpot picks on this list.

Single motor or dual motor — does it matter?

Dual motors lift more weight, run quieter, and stay more stable at full height, which is why they anchor the premium picks here. Single-motor budget desks are fine for a laptop-plus-monitor setup, just keep the load modest.

How much weight should my standing desk hold?

Add up everything that lives on the desk: monitors, arms, a PC tower, speakers. Budget desks here handle 110–176 lb; heavy multi-monitor rigs want 220–355 lb of rated capacity for real stability.

Are standing desk converters worth it?

Yes, if you already own a solid desk — a riser like the VIVO DESK-V000K gives you sit-stand switching without replacing furniture. If you want clean cable management and maximum stability, a full electric desk is the better long-term bet.
