Before you buy a Halloween costume

Will it arrive before Halloween?

Check the delivery estimate on the Amazon listing before you order — Prime-eligible items usually show a date at checkout. Popular sizes sell through in October, so earlier beats later.

How do costume sizes run?

Costume sizing varies wildly by brand — always check the size chart on the listing and read the fit notes. When in doubt, size up: nobody ever complained a costume was too comfortable.

Are these the officially licensed versions?

Where the listing says officially licensed (Rubie's, Fun World, and others on this page), you're getting the studio-approved design — not a lookalike.

What accessories do I still need?

Each card lists what's in the box. Masks, wigs, makeup, and prop weapons are usually sold separately — check the listing's "frequently bought together" section.
