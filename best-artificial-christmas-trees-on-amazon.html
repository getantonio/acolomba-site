Before you buy an artificial Christmas tree

What size artificial Christmas tree should I buy?

Match the tree to your ceiling and your floor space. A 7.5-foot tree fits comfortably under a 9-foot ceiling with room for a topper; a 6.5-foot tree suits standard 8-foot ceilings. Also check the base width: a full-profile tree can span 59 inches across, while slim models like the Kingswood Fir Slim on this list need only 30 inches.

Should I get a pre-lit or unlit artificial tree?

Pre-lit trees arrive with the lights already strung through the branches — no annual untangling of light strings, and setup is mostly shaping. Unlit trees cost you an afternoon of stringing but give you full control over the light style, color, and count. If you love heirloom or specialty light sets, go unlit; if you want plug-and-go decorating, go pre-lit.

How do I make an artificial tree look fuller?

The shaping step is what separates a showroom tree from a sad one. After assembly, spend 45 minutes to an hour peeling the branches back and pulling the branch tips apart to fill gaps — the setup guidance for trees like the Kingswood Fir Slim recommends exactly that. Fluff from the bottom up, step back to check your progress often, and let the hinged branches settle for a day before the final tweaks.
