Before you decorate for Halloween

How far apart should I space the signs?

Most of these sets are designed for cluster placement — two to four feet apart reads as one scene instead of scattered props. Line a walkway or frame your porch, and keep glow-in-the-dark pieces where they'll catch daylight to charge.

Will they survive rain and wind?

All ten picks use weather-resistant or waterproof materials — corrugated plastic, PP plastic, or metal — and come with stakes. Push stakes firmly into the ground, and bring metal or corrugated pieces in during severe storms.

When should I put them up?

Anytime in October works. The glow-in-the-dark sets need a few daylight hours to charge, so give them an afternoon of sun before trick-or-treat night for maximum effect.
