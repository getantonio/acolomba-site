The Brain Shift Method

Seven languages for rehearsing a better response

Most of the behavior you want to change is not a single bad decision. It is a loop: a trigger, a response, a quick payoff, and a cost that arrives late. The Brain Shift Method is a practical, self-directed system for doing something about it — define the loop in four lines, compress it into thirteen anchor words, and write fifty present-tense statements.

This second edition expands the method into seven languages of change — I am, I feel, I do, I love, I speak, I see, I know — so you can approach the same behavior from every angle that matters. No streaks. No scores. No device watching you. Just paper, attention, and repetition: fourteen cycles to a practiced response that meets the loop sooner.
