Halloween Projector Lights FAQs

How far from the house should a Halloween projector go?

Stake or wall-mount the projector 10–27 feet from the surface for most models — farther means a bigger, dimmer image. Aim at a light-colored wall, garage door, or window; dark surfaces wash the projection out. Test it one night before the party to dial in focus and angle.

Can I leave a Halloween projector outside in the rain?

Yes — most of these are built for it. Look for IP44, IP45 or IP65 waterproof ratings on the listing. The low-voltage adapter or plug is often NOT waterproof, so keep that connection under cover or inside a weatherproof outlet box.

What's the difference between a slide projector and a movie projector?

Slides project static or slowly-rotating single images (ghosts, skulls, pumpkins) — simple, cheap, and very bright. Movie projectors play actual animated scenes through a window screen, which looks far more realistic from the street but needs a dark room and a screen or sheer curtain.

Do I have to turn the projector on and off every night?

Most slide-style projectors run on a standard plug with an auto-off timer (2/4/6 hours, or 6-on/18-off), so they handle themselves all night. For true hands-off haunted windows, pick a model with an auto-start timer so it fires up the same time every evening.
