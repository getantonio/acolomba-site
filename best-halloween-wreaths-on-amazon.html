Halloween-wreath questions, answered

Will these wreaths survive on an uncovered front door?

Several wreaths on this list are listed for both indoor and outdoor use, and battery-powered LED lights mean no wiring is exposed to the elements. Still, wreaths last longest on a covered porch or under an overhang — one seller advises keeping its wreath out of damp spots and direct sunlight to prolong its life. If your door takes weather head-on, bring fabric and mesh wreaths inside during storms and they’ll look great for years.

What size wreath fits a standard front door?

Most wreaths here run 20 to 24 inches across, which suits a standard front door nicely; the two 26-inch licensed wreaths make a bolder statement on wider double doors. Many mesh and fabric wreaths ship tightly packed, so fluff and spread the branches after unpacking — several listings note you’ll get the fullest look that way.

How do I store a Halloween wreath so I can reuse it next year?

Cool, dry, and crush-free: one wreath on this list ships with its own zippered storage bag, and any sturdy wreath box or large bag works for the rest. Hang the wreath rather than stacking things on it so mesh and foliage keep their shape, and give the mesh a quick fluff next October before you hang it again.
