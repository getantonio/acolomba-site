Gingerbread House Kit FAQs

Pre-baked or bake-it-yourself — which should I buy?

Pre-baked kits are the practical pick for most families: the panels are ready, so the fun starts at assembly instead of at the oven. Bake-it-yourself kits suit builders who want the baking itself to be part of the tradition.

How do I keep a gingerbread house from collapsing?

Use the icing as glue, let the walls set before adding the roof, and use a tray or roof holder where the kit includes one. Several kits here include trays and roof supporters for exactly that reason.

How many houses do I need for a group?

One house works for a small family decorating together. For a party or classroom, look at the multi-house sets here — two-house duos for pairs, and four-house village sets for larger groups.

Are gingerbread house kits good gifts?

Yes — they are one of the safest holiday gifts, because they suit any age and double as both an activity and a centerpiece. A mini village set is the gift for a family; the glittery duo is the gift for a household that loves a little sparkle.
