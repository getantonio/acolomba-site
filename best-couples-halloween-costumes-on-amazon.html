Before you buy a couples Halloween costume

Are these really two costumes?

Every entry above is a single listing that dresses two adults — we checked each product page and dropped one popular option whose own reviews suggested only one costume body was included. Read the "what's included" bullets on any listing before you buy, especially when a price looks too good for two.

What sizes do couples costumes come in?

Most foam and tunic sets (PB&J, plug & socket, loofah & soap) come in Standard one-size or S–XXL ranges. One-size tunics run loose by design; if you are between sizes on a sized set, size up — foam does not stretch.

How do I wash a foam costume?

Hand wash cold and line dry, per the care labels on the listings above. Do not machine wash or tumble dry foam costumes — heat warps them.

Why buy on Amazon instead of a costume shop?

Prime delivery before Halloween and free returns on most listings. Costume-shop prices on the same sets are rarely lower, and the review counts above (up to 1,910 on the plug & socket set) are real buyer feedback you do not get at a pop-up store.
