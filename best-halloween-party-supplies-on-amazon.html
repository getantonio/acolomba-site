Before you buy Halloween party supplies

How many place settings do I need for a Halloween party?

Count one full place setting per guest — dinner plate, dessert plate, cup, and napkin — then add a few extras for spills and seconds. Most all-in-one tableware kits (like the 30-guest Sliner set on this list) state their piece counts on the listing, so match the kit to your guest list and round up rather than down.

Can I reuse paper Halloween decorations, or are they one-and-done?

It depends on the material. Foil/mylar banners and thick card-stock props are designed to be reused — the listings for the PERFQU balloon banner and KatchOn photo props both say so. Thin paper streamers, disposable tableware, and latex balloon garlands are generally single-use. If you want decorations that come back next October, look for "reusable" in the listing.

How early should I order party supplies for Halloween?

Decorations and themed tableware sell through fast in October, and shipping windows tighten the week before the 31st. Ordering a few weeks ahead gives you time to check the box for missing pieces (balloon kits and prop sets have many small parts) and reorder if anything arrives damaged.
