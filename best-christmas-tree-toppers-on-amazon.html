Christmas Tree Topper FAQs

What size tree topper should I buy?

Match the topper to your tree: 7 to 8 inches suits tabletop and slim trees, while 10 to 16 inches fits standard 6 to 9 foot trees. A topper that's too large can look top-heavy; too small and it disappears into the branches.

How do lighted tree toppers get power?

Three ways: plug-in (a lead wire runs down the tree to an outlet), battery-operated (many now include timers so they light up automatically each evening), and USB-powered (handy if you have a nearby port or power bank). Battery and USB models keep cords off your tree.

Will a heavy topper tip my tree over?

Most toppers mount with a spiral cone or clamp base that grips the top branch, and lightweight wire or plastic designs weigh only a few ounces. Save heavier glass finials for sturdy, well-anchored trees and check that the top branch is straight and strong.

Star or angel — which topper is more traditional?

Both go back centuries: the star represents the Star of Bethlehem, while the angel recalls the angels of the nativity story. Stars read classic and versatile; angels add elegance and a focal figure. Snowflakes, finials, and bows are newer twists that work with modern themes.
