Before you buy a snow shovel

Pusher or shovel — which do I need?

Pushers move light snow fast across driveways; shovels lift and throw deeper snow. Combo blades do a bit of both — most suburban driveways are happiest with a combo, or one of each.

What blade width should I get?

18–20 inches is the sweet spot for most people. Wider blades (26–48") clear faster but get heavy when the snow is wet — match the width to your strength and your snowfall.

Will these scratch my deck or pavers?

Poly blades with nylon or aluminum wear strips are the safe choice for composite decks and pavers. Bare steel blades can mark surfaces, so check before you scrape.

How do I avoid hurting my back?

Push instead of lifting whenever you can, keep loads small, and consider a spring-assist or wheeled design. Your spine will thank you in February.
