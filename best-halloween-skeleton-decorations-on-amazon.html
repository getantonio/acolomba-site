Before you build your skeleton army

Will these survive outside in October weather?

Most picks on this list are plastic and made for outdoor display, including the groundbreakers and life-size figures. Animated or lighted pieces need batteries kept dry; bring electronics inside during heavy rain and they’ll last season after season.

How do I make a graveyard scene look good?

Layer heights: a tall life-size skeleton in back, groundbreakers crawling in front, and smaller sets scattered mid-ground. Add dim spotlights or the glowing-eyed pieces for night, and keep sightlines open from the street so trick-or-treaters get the full scene.
