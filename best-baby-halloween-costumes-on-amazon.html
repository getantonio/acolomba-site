Baby costume questions, answered

How should I size a baby Halloween costume?

Costume sizing runs looser and less predictably than everyday clothes. Measure your baby's height and check the listing's size chart rather than going by age alone — when you're between sizes, size up so a warm onesie fits underneath on a cold October night.

Will these be warm enough for trick-or-treating?

Flannel and fleece picks are the warmest on this list; thin polyester rompers are lighter and breathe better. Whatever you choose, layer a long-sleeve onesie underneath and keep outdoor time short for newborns.

When should we do the photo shoot?

A day or two before Halloween, in the morning after a nap — not on the big night itself. Costumes and cameras both go smoother when baby is rested, and you still get the full October backdrop.
