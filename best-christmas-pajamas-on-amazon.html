Christmas Pajama FAQs

What size should I order in matching family pajamas?

Check each listing's own size chart — family sets here span separate men, women, kids, and baby charts, and several are snug-fitting by design. When a size falls between two options, the roomier choice is usually the safer Christmas-morning bet.

Are these pajamas machine washable?

Most sets here are machine washable per their listings, with a few exceptions: the reindeer-and-elf family set is hand wash only, and the teddy fleece onesie is machine wash cold with tumble dry low. Always check the individual listing's care instructions.

Do matching sets include the dog?

Two picks here cover pets: the organic tartan family set offers a unisex pets option, and the buffalo plaid dog pajamas are sized for dogs — including large breeds in the XL size, with neck and chest measurements in the listing.

Onesie or two-piece set — which is better?

Onesies are warmer and simpler for kids and cold houses, and the fleece onesie here is the cozy pick. Two-piece button-down sets look more polished in photos and are easier for bathroom trips — the classic plaid and women's sets here are the photo-ready options.
