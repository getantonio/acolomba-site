LED strip light FAQs

What does RGBIC actually mean?

RGBIC strips have independent control chips along the line, so each segment can show a different color at the same time — flowing rainbows, chasing effects, multicolor scenes. Plain RGB shows one color across the whole strip at a time. Both are listed on Amazon, so check which one you're buying.

Do I need WiFi, or is Bluetooth enough?

Bluetooth-only strips work fine from your phone, but they lose voice control, out-of-home control, and (usually) Alexa/Google integration. If you want voice commands or automations, get a WiFi model.

Will the strip stay stuck to my wall?

The included 3M adhesive holds best on clean, flat, hard surfaces. Wipe the surface with isopropyl alcohol first, and on textured walls or fresh paint consider the mounting clips many of these kits include.

How long a run can I power from one adapter?

Stick to the length in the box — long runs fed from one end can dim toward the far end. For very long installations, plan on powering both ends or adding a second adapter rather than daisy-chaining past the manufacturer's max.
