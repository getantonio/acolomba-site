      How to play — new here? Start here
      1. Read a clue, then type the answer into the matching squares — one letter per square.
      2. Tap a square to select it; tap it again to switch between Across and Down.
      3. The small number in a square’s corner matches the clue number.
      4. Use Check letters to verify your work, or Reveal puzzle if you’re truly stuck.
