How to play — new here? Start here
  1. Fill the grid so every row, every column, and each 3×3 box contains the digits 1–9 exactly once.
  2. The numbers already placed are your starting clues — they never change.
  3. Use logic, not guessing: look for rows, columns, or boxes that are nearly complete.
  4. Tap a square, then tap a number (or just type). Toggle Notes to pencil in candidates — mistakes light up in red.
