Halloween Ghost Decorations FAQs

Will these ghost decorations survive October weather?

It depends on the pick. The metal, waterproof-fabric, and UL-listed outdoor items on this list are built for rain and wind; the window curtains and lighter hanging ghosts do best under a covered porch or indoors on stormy nights. Check each listing for its outdoor rating, and stake or tether anything tall.

Are the lighted ghosts battery-operated or plug-in?

Both kinds made the list. The hanging ghost sets run on AA batteries with auto timers, so they work anywhere with no outlet. The string lights and inflatables plug into a standard outlet — no batteries to replace mid-season.

How big is a 12-foot inflatable ghost, really?

About as tall as a one-story home's eaves — it will dominate an average front yard. Measure your space before ordering anything over 5 feet tall, and keep inflatables a few feet back from walkways so trick-or-treaters don't trip on the tethers.

Can I reuse these decorations next Halloween?

Yes, with basic care. Deflate and fully dry inflatables before storing, fold fabric ghosts instead of cramming them into a bin, and remove batteries from battery-operated picks during the off-season to prevent corrosion.
