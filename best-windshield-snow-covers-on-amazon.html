Windshield snow cover FAQs

Do windshield snow covers actually work?

For overnight snow and frost, yes — the cover takes the ice instead of your windshield, and a single pull exposes clear glass underneath. They're best on flat windshields; heavily curved glass can leave edge gaps where snow sneaks in.

Magnetic vs elastic vs straps — which secures best?

Magnetic edges sit flush and are quickest to place, but wind can lift loose edges. Elastic bands around the mirrors are fast and secure enough for most weather. Straps that hook to the wheels or mirrors are the most storm-proof, especially on exposed driveways. Most covers on this list use at least two of the three.

Will a cover keep my wipers from freezing?

The best designs have wiper-blade covers or flaps — otherwise lift the wipers and lay the cover over them. Either way, the blade stays off the glass instead of freezing to it.

What size cover do I need?

Measure your windshield. Standard covers fit most sedans; if you drive an SUV or truck, check the listing's dimensions — every pick on this page lists its size, and several come in multiple sizes.
