10 Best Halloween Candy on Amazon (2026) FAQs

How much Halloween candy do you actually need for one night?

Nobody can give you an exact number, because it depends entirely on your block. If you're on a quiet street, a 55- to 110-count bag covers you easily. If you're on a busy street with sidewalk traffic all night, start at 160+ pieces and go up from there. Buy the bigger bag — leftover candy is a feature, not a failure.

When should you buy Halloween candy on Amazon?

Earlier than you think. Buy it in early-to-mid October so it arrives with days to spare, not the morning of the 31st. Chocolate varieties are worth ordering before any heat spike or shipping delay can melt them into modern art. Ordering early also means you get the good bulk bags before seasonal listings run low.

Which candy disappears fastest from the trick-or-treat bowl?

At most doors, the chocolate variety packs and peanut-butter cups are the first to disappear — that's why variety bags built around Mars and Hershey classics are the safe bet. But plenty of kids go straight for the fruity and sour stuff, so a bag that mixes chocolate with chewy/fruity pieces keeps every visitor happy.

How do you keep bulk Halloween candy fresh?

Keep bulk bags sealed in a cool, dry place until the big night — a pantry or closet works fine. Individually wrapped pieces stay fresh right in the bag. Avoid the garage if it gets warm, because chocolate will bloom or melt. Once the bag is opened, reseal it or transfer pieces to an airtight container and you've got weeks of snacks.
