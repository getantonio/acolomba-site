Air fryer FAQs

What size air fryer do I need?

For one or two people, 2-4 quarts is plenty. A 4-6 quart basket handles family meals, and dual-basket models let you cook two things at different temperatures at once. Measure your counter first — the bigger ovens are genuinely huge.

Are air fryers actually healthier than frying?

They use a fraction of the oil a deep fryer needs, which is where most of the calorie savings come from. The food itself isn't magic — a frozen fry is still a frozen fry — but the oil difference is real.

Do air fryers work as well as ovens?

For small batches they're faster than a full-size oven and crisp better because the hot air circulates in a small space. For a whole turkey or a sheet-pan dinner, the oven still wins.

How do I clean an air fryer?

Most baskets and crisper plates are dishwasher-safe; a soak in hot soapy water handles the rest. Line the bottom with parchment made for air fryers to cut down on stuck-on mess.
