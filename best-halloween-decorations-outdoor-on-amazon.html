Before you decorate for Halloween

Are these Halloween decorations safe to leave outside all season?

Most outdoor-rated inflatables, projectors, and stake lights are built weather-resistant — look for IP44 or IP65 waterproof ratings in the specs. Still, stake or tether large items, keep plugs off standing water, and move animatronic props under cover during heavy storms to extend their life.

How do I keep giant inflatables from blowing away?

Use every included ground stake and tether rope, and set up on a calm day before the season's wind picks up. Placing large inflatables near a fence or hedge as a windbreak helps, and always plug the blower into a GFCI-protected outdoor outlet.

Do solar Halloween pathway lights work on cloudy fall days?

Solar lights charge in daylight, not just direct sun, but overcast days mean shorter run times at night. Face the panel south for maximum exposure, and keep it away from porch or street lights so the dusk sensor triggers properly after dark.
