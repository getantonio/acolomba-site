Halloween Banner FAQs

What size Halloween banner fits a standard front door?

Porch banners around 12 x 71 inches are made to flank a standard door — one on each side of the frame. Garden flags are smaller, typically 12 x 18 inches, and hang from a garden pole or porch stand instead.

Can Halloween banners stay outside all October?

Fabric banners in polyester or Oxford cloth with weather-resistant finishes are made for outdoor use; check the listing for a weatherproof or outdoor rating. Lighted banners need their electrical connections kept out of standing water, and indoor-only card or foil streamers should stay under cover.

How do you hang a porch banner without damaging the door frame?

Most porch banners come pre-assembled with a rod and string or ribbon — hang them from a hook, nail, or adhesive hook on the frame. The grommets at the bottom of many banners let you add small weights so they hang straight instead of swinging.

What is the difference between a porch banner and a garden flag?

Porch banners are tall vertical signs that hang beside or on a door, while garden flags are smaller 12 x 18 inch flags on a pole or stand in the yard. Bunting is a third style: a strung line of flags for fences, rails, and party walls.
