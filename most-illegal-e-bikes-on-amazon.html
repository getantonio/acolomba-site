The legal fine print

Is it illegal to buy these?

No. Every bike on this list is sold legally on Amazon. The issue is where and how you ride it. Most US states define an e-bike as 750W or less with pedal-assist cutoffs at 20 MPH (Class 1/2) or 28 MPH (Class 3). Above that, you're typically in moped or motorcycle territory.

What does that actually mean for me?

Depending on your state: possible requirements for registration, insurance, a driver's license, or plates — and possible bans from bike lanes, bike paths, and trails. Some states are lenient; some impound first and ask later. Check your state DMV and local ordinances before you ride.

Why does Amazon sell them then?

Because selling a high-powered e-bike isn't illegal — riding one in the wrong place is. Many buyers use them on private land, and enforcement varies wildly. The responsibility is on the rider, which is why you're reading this.
