Witch Hat FAQs

How do I keep a witch hat from falling off?

Most wearable witch hats include an elastic chin band or inner headband — check the listing photos for one. If yours is loose, a couple of bobby pins through the inner band into your hair, or a strip of foam weather-sealing tape inside the brim, will lock it down for a full night of trick-or-treating.

What is the difference between costume witch hats and decorative ones?

Costume hats are sized to wear: adult or kids’ sizing, chin straps or headbands, and fabrics like felt or velvet. Decorative witch hats are smaller, lighter, and made to hang — multi-packs with nylon cord are designed to string across porches and ceilings, not to sit on a head.

Are light-up witch hats safe to hang outdoors?

Check the listing for a waterproof or outdoor rating before you hang anything outside. Waterproof light-up witch hats are built for porches, gardens, and yards; indoor-only sets should stay under cover. As always, keep electrical connections out of standing water.

Which witch hats are best for a group or classroom?

Multi-packs. The 12-piece sets on this list dress a whole classroom, party, or couples’ costume crew for a few dollars per hat, and several include bonus bat stickers for decorating the room too.
