Before you buy heated gloves

How do heated gloves actually work?

Thin heating elements are woven across the back of the hand and fingers, powered by rechargeable battery packs (usually 7.4V) tucked into the cuff. Three heat settings let you trade warmth for runtime — low can last most of a day, high drains a pack in a few hours.

How long do the batteries last between charges?

It depends on the setting. Most rechargeable pairs list 2–3 hours on high and 6–8 hours on low; AA-powered gloves run about 4+ hours per set of batteries. Carry a spare pack if you're out all day.

Can I use my phone while wearing them?

Many pairs on this list include touchscreen-compatible fingertips, so you can check a map or answer a call without exposing a hand. Gloves without that feature usually need to come off for phone use.
