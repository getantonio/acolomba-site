Robot vacuum FAQs

Are self-emptying robot vacuums worth it?

If you hate touching the dustbin, yes — the dock holds weeks of debris and the bags trap dust better than emptying a little bin into the trash every day. The dock itself needs a bag change every couple of months.

Can robot vacuums handle pet hair?

The good ones can, especially with rubber brush rollers that resist tangles better than bristle brushes. Long hair will still wrap around anything eventually — check the brush design on each pick.

Do I need LiDAR or is a camera enough?

LiDAR maps faster and works in the dark; camera-based navigation is usually cheaper and handles most homes fine. Both beat the old bump-and-spin robots at not missing rooms.

Will a robot vacuum replace my upright?

For daily maintenance, mostly yes. For deep-pile carpet and stairs, keep the upright — robots maintain floors between deep cleans rather than replacing them entirely.
