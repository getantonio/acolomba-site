Before you buy a Halloween makeup kit

Will the makeup actually last through Halloween night?

For the whole night, yes — oil-based and waterproof formulas hold up far longer than water-based palettes. The professional kits above are built to resist smudging, cracking, and sweat. Setting with a translucent powder or setting spray extends wear even further.

Is Halloween makeup safe for kids' skin?

Stick to kits labeled non-toxic and hypoallergenic, and avoid applying anything around the eyes and mouth unless the product specifically says it's eye-safe. A patch test on the inside of the arm 24 hours before the party is the pro move — especially for kids with sensitive skin.

How do you get it all off afterward?

For the basics: face paint palettes, crayons, fake blood, scar wax, and liquid latex remove easily with warm water and soap or a gentle oil-based cleanser. Pro FX products (pros-aide, rigid collodion) can need dedicated removers — check the instructions before applying.
