FAQs

Are laser light projectors safe?

These are consumer-grade decorative lasers. As with any laser, don't stare directly into the beam, and mount the projector where kids and pets can't look straight into it.

How much area does one projector cover?

The picks here range from 3,200 to 5,000 square feet — roughly a full house front plus the yard — measured from about 33 feet away. Check the coverage spec on each card and place the projector accordingly.

Do they work in rain and snow?

Most picks here carry an IP65 weatherproof rating with aluminum housings, and a few are explicitly freeze-proof. The ground stake handles lawns; several also include an indoor base or wall mount.
