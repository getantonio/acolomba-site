Ornament questions, answered

Shatterproof or glass — which should I get?

Kids, pets, and high-traffic rooms point to shatterproof plastic: it bounces instead of shattering and the sets on this list look far better than the price suggests. Go glass when the ornaments are the point — mercury-glass and vintage minis read like heirlooms because they are built to last.

How many ornaments do I need for a full tree?

A common rule of thumb is about 10 ornaments per foot of tree — so a 7-foot tree wants roughly 70. The 100-piece set at the top of this list fills a big tree on its own; mix it with a smaller set for texture and you're done.

Can the LED ornaments go outside?

Some can: the outdoor LED ball lights and the giant inflatable ornaments on this list are built weatherproof for porches and yards. Standard indoor ornaments — glass and acrylic — should stay inside.
