Christmas Mug FAQs

What size Christmas mug is best for cocoa?

A 15 to 18-ounce mug is the cocoa sweet spot — room for cocoa plus whipped cream or marshmallows without sloshing. The camper mugs here are 15 ounces and the Lenox cocoa mug is 18; the 10-ounce Spode mugs suit smaller servings and kids.

Are Christmas mugs dishwasher and microwave safe?

Every mug on this list is listed as microwave and dishwasher safe, including the porcelain and stoneware sets. Handcrafted stoneware still benefits from a gentle cycle, and gold accents — like the Lenox holly trim — last longest if you skip harsh detergents.

Is a mug set or a single mug the better Christmas gift?

A set wins when the recipient hosts: the Spode set of four and the ZAROCRUS set of six cover a whole table. A single statement mug — a camper mug or a handcrafted Scandi mug — wins when the gift is personal, for one person's morning ritual.

Do Christmas mugs make good stocking stuffers?

Yes, with one caveat: a 15 or 16-ounce camper or stoneware mug is a tight fit for a standard stocking, so pair it with a packet of cocoa and let the mug be the gift that peeks out the top. The smaller 10-ounce Spode mugs fit more easily.
