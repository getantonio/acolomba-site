Christmas Cookie Cutter FAQs

Stainless steel or plastic cutters — which should I buy?

Stainless steel is the long-haul pick: it holds a sharp edge, resists bending on thick dough, and lasts for years. Plastic mini cutters, like the 30-piece set here, are the party pick — light, cheap in bulk, and great for kids.

What size cookie cutter is best for Christmas cookies?

Most sets here run about 2 to 4 inches, which is the sweet spot: big enough to decorate, small enough to bake evenly. Mini cutters around 1 inch are for party bites and garnishes, not the main cookie tray.

How do you keep cookie cutters from sticking to dough?

Chill the dough well, dust the cutter lightly with flour, and press straight down without twisting. Stainless sets with smooth edges, like the ones here, release cleaner than rough-edged cutters.

Do cookie cutters make good gifts?

Yes — they are one of the easiest Christmas gifts for a baker, because they suit any skill level. The JOYIN set with its rolling pin is the starter gift; the Ann Clark USA-made set is the gift for the baker who already has everything.
