Outdoor Christmas Lights — FAQ

What's the difference between smart and regular outdoor Christmas lights?

Smart lights connect over Wi-Fi and are controlled with a phone app or voice assistant. Budget strings use a remote or plug-in timer instead. Both are fine outdoors as long as the lights themselves carry an outdoor waterproof rating like IP44 or IP65.

Can I leave these lights out in rain and snow?

Look for the IP rating. IP44 handles splashes, IP65 and IP67 handle heavy rain and snow. Keep power adapters and plugs sheltered or inside a watertight box when the instructions call for it.

How many feet of lights do I need for my house?

Start by measuring the space — eaves, bushes, or trees — then check each product's total lit length. Connectable sets let you daisy-chain strands, which is handy for long rooflines.
