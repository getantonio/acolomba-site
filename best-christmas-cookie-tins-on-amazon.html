Christmas Cookie Tin FAQs

Which cookie tin is the classic Christmas gift?

The Royal Dansk blue tin — the 24-ounce Danish butter cookie tin has been the default holiday cookie gift for generations, and it comes in 12-ounce and 48-ounce sizes too.

Should I buy a filled cookie tin or an empty one?

Buy filled when you want a ready-to-give gift — Royal Dansk, Walker's, and Mrs. Fields tins all arrive with cookies inside. Buy empty when you bake: the Steel Mill & Co and KIBAGA sets are sturdy tins with lids made for filling with your own cookies.

How long do cookies stay fresh in a tin?

Butter cookies and shortbread in a sealed tin typically stay fresh for several weeks unopened; once opened, keep the lid on and store in a cool, dry place. Check each listing's best-by date when it arrives.

Do cookie tins make good gifts for coworkers?

Yes — a sealed tin is easy to hand over, share, and store on a desk. The 12-ounce Royal Dansk tin and the 30-nibbler Mrs. Fields tin are sized for a single recipient, while the 48-ounce tin suits a whole office.
