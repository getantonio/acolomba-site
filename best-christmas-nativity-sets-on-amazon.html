Before you buy a nativity set

Indoor or outdoor?

Porcelain, hand-painted resin, and ivory figurine sets belong indoors, where they stay safe from weather and curious hands. PVC board silhouettes, metal yard signs, and inflatables are built for the lawn — look for waterproof materials and included stakes if your winters get rough.

What size should you get?

For a mantel or tabletop, a 5- to 9-inch figure scale is the sweet spot — big enough to see across the room, small enough to leave room for garland. For a yard, 4 feet is the minimum that reads from the street; 6 to 8 feet turns the whole display into a landmark.

Should you start small or buy the full scene?

Collections like Willow Tree and Fontanini are designed to grow: start with the Holy Family core, then add shepherds, animals, and Wise Men in later years. Complete sets like the 11- and 12-piece picks above give you the whole story in one box.
