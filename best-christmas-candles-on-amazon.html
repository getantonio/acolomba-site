Christmas Candle FAQs

Which Christmas candle scent should I start with?

If you only buy one, start with the family you love most: fir and balsam for a fresh-tree house, Christmas Cookie or cinnamon for a baking house, and a crackling wood-wick candle if you want the fireplace feeling without a fireplace.

How long do these Christmas candles burn?

The 22-ounce Yankee jars are listed at up to 150 hours and the NEST three-wick at up to 100 hours, while the WoodWick trilogy is listed at up to 50 hours — always check the listing for the exact size you buy, since burn time varies by jar.

Are three-wick candles better than single-wick?

Three wicks melt the wax pool faster and throw scent further, which suits open-plan rooms. Single-wick large jars burn longer and suit bedrooms and smaller rooms — both kinds appear on this list.

Do Christmas candles make good gifts?

Yes — a candle is the safest holiday gift there is, and the six-pack set here splits into six separate gifts. For a single impressive gift, the NEST Holiday three-wick in its gold vessel is the luxury pick.
