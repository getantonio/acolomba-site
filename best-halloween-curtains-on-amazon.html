Halloween Curtain FAQs

What size Halloween curtains do I need for a standard window?

Measure the window first, then match the listing size: common panel sizes here run 40 x 84, 42 x 84, and 52 x 63 inches. Panels are usually sold in pairs, so a 2-panel set covers one average window; wider windows may need two sets.

Are Halloween lace curtains sheer or private?

Lace panels like the spider-web and black lace styles here are light-filtering and sheer — they dress the window without blocking daylight. For privacy or darkness, pick a blackout style such as the fall forest or death moth drapes.

Can Halloween shower curtains go in the washing machine?

Many polyester shower curtains are machine washable — check the individual listing. Several on this list state washable and waterproof polyester, and most include their own hooks and rust-proof eyelets or grommets.

Do Halloween curtains work after October?

Many do. Black lace, gothic velvet, and fall forest blackout styles read as year-round goth or autumn decor, while the cartoon ghost and pumpkin shower curtains are best swapped out when the season ends.
