Before you buy a 40+ MPH e-bike

Are these actually legal to ride?

It depends where you live. Most US states follow the 3-class e-bike system: 750W max and 20–28 MPH. Everything on this list blows past those limits, which means many of these are legally mopeds or motorcycles in your state — registration, insurance, or plates may be required, and bike paths and trails may be off-limits. Check your state and local laws before you ride.

Are the top speeds real?

Speeds above are the manufacturers' claimed specs as shown on their Amazon listings, usually measured with a light rider on flat ground. Real-world top speed varies with rider weight, terrain, wind, and battery charge.

Why buy on Amazon instead of direct?

Amazon's return policy and buyer protection. With marketplace sellers, check the seller's ratings and read recent reviews before committing — a $2,000 bike deserves five minutes of due diligence.
