Data collection

The current app does not transmit, sell, or share personal data. Practice progress, journal check-ins, optional notes and goals, and app preferences are stored locally on the device so the app can remember them.

Accounts

The current app does not use accounts or login.

Analytics and tracking

The current app does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking.

Audio and recordings

Built-in audio files are bundled with the app and play locally on the device. Personal recordings and user-selected imported audio stay in local on-device storage and are not uploaded by Brain Shift. They remain there unless the user removes them, the app, or its data.

Subscriptions and payments

Brain Shift offers an optional auto-renewable Plus subscription through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payments and maintains purchase records under the Apple Media Services Terms and Apple's privacy policy. The app reads StoreKit subscription status to unlock Plus features; Brain Shift does not receive or store payment card details.

Contact

For privacy questions, email getantonio@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy will be updated before release if the app adds accounts, analytics, custom voice generation, cloud sync, or other data features that change how information is handled.
