Christmas Sweater FAQs

What makes a sweater an 'ugly' Christmas sweater?

The formula is loud on purpose: bold holiday motifs, clashing colors, and extra touches like 3D trim, pom poms, or bells. The picks here range from classic fair isle knits to light-up trees — pick your level of loud.

Do light-up Christmas sweaters need batteries?

Yes — light-up sweaters run on small battery packs, usually tucked in a pocket. Check each listing for its battery type and whether batteries are included before party night.

How do I pick a size in a matching family sweater?

Check each listing's own size chart — family sets here span separate adult and kids charts, and several knits are loose-fitting by design. When in doubt between sizes, the roomier choice is usually the safer party bet.

Can I wash a light-up or 3D sweater?

Plain knits are generally machine washable per their listings, but light-up sweaters need their battery packs removed first, and 3D details like bells and pom poms do best with gentler care. Always check the individual listing's care instructions.
